FAQ

Questions before you commit.

Clear answers about ownership, timelines, language, pricing and support.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Domain, hosting, source code, content and credentials are yours on launch day.

How fast can a website go live?

A single-page presence can be live in 48 hours. A standard launch page is typically 3–5 working days, depending on content and feedback.

Can you work in Hausa?

Yes. English and Hausa are supported. Bilingual integration is scoped per project.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. SARARI Digital is remote and can serve clients internationally.

What payment structure do you use?

The standard structure is 50% deposit to start and 50% on delivery, with the exact arrangement confirmed in the project scope.

What happens after launch?

Website projects include 30 days of support; custom software projects include 90 days unless the scope says otherwise.
